'Till All Are One — A Hearts of Iron IV Mod



A Transformers total-conversion project for Hearts of Iron IV, originally developed for HOI4 version 1.16.



The project was designed to transform the setting of Hearts of Iron IV into a unique interpretation of Cybertron, featuring a custom world map, original political structures, custom ideologies, 3D models, interface elements, and the foundations for a heavily narrative-driven conflict between the Autobots and Decepticons.



This repository represents the project in its unfinished and experimental state. Development has ended, but the existing work is being preserved for anyone interested in exploring, modifying, or continuing it.



\---



Current Content



The current version contains several of the foundational systems and assets intended for the full project.



Custom Cybertron Map



The mod includes a custom world map created specifically for its Cybertron setting.



The map was intended to serve as more than a replacement for the vanilla HOI4 world. Its geography and political divisions were designed around the mod's own factions, conflicts, and narrative rather than attempting to adapt Cybertron directly to Earth's geopolitical structure.



Custom Countries and Factions



The setting contains original implementations of Cybertronian states and political factions, with the two central powers being:



\- Autobot Commonwealth — District of Iacon

\- Decepticon Empire — Megatron's Revolt



These factions were intended to become the two principal playable powers and eventual participants in the final global conflict.



Additional states and factions existed primarily to support the geopolitical situation, proxy conflicts, and narrative surrounding these two powers.



Custom Ideologies



The project includes custom political ideologies designed around the setting.



Rather than relying exclusively on the political categories of vanilla Hearts of Iron IV, these were intended to represent the different political movements, governments, and philosophical divisions emerging across Cybertron.



3D Models



The project integrates custom 3D content into Hearts of Iron IV, including models adapted for use within the game.



Some source models originated from Sketchfab and were used under their respective Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) licenses.



The original authors and licenses of third-party assets remain applicable to those assets. Any continuation or redistribution of the project should preserve the attribution and licensing requirements associated with them.



User Interface



The mod also contains custom and modified interface elements intended to establish its own visual identity and move the experience away from the presentation of vanilla Hearts of Iron IV.



Original Setting and Continuity



The mod does not attempt to directly reproduce one specific existing Transformers continuity.



Instead, it features an original continuity and lore created specifically for this project.



Characters, factions, political circumstances, conflicts, and historical events were intended to reinterpret familiar Transformers concepts within a setting designed around grand strategy and interactive narrative.

Original Vision



Although development never reached the point where the complete concept could be implemented, the project was originally designed around an unusual idea for a Hearts of Iron IV mod:



A Narrative 1v1 Grand Strategy Experience



At its core, the mod was intended to be a two-player / two-faction narrative strategy experience.



The two primary playable sides would have been:



Autobot Commonwealth



District of Iacon



and



Decepticon Empire



Megatron's Revolt



Instead of designing every country on the map as an equally developed playable nation, the majority of the experience would revolve around these two major powers.



The fundamental structure was:

Both factions would compete for influence across the planet through political actions, interventions, regional conflicts, and narrative proxy wars.



The concept was partially inspired by the narrative approach of mods such as The New Order, where geopolitical events are used as vehicles for storytelling rather than existing exclusively as traditional HOI4 warfare.



However, the central difference was that the entire geopolitical system would ultimately support a deliberate 1v1 strategic rivalry.



\---



Proxy Wars



Before direct confrontation became inevitable, the Autobots and Decepticons would compete indirectly.



Regional conflicts across Cybertron could become proxy wars in which both sides supported opposing factions.



Their outcomes could influence:



\- Political control

\- Resources

\- Strategic territory

\- Military strength

\- Future allies

\- Narrative events

\- The balance of power between Autobots and Decepticons



These conflicts were intended to make the rivalry develop gradually rather than immediately beginning with a conventional world war.



Player decisions during this period would shape the circumstances under which the final conflict occurred.



\---



Multiple Narrative Paths



Although the ultimate confrontation between the two major factions was intended to be unavoidable, the political development of each faction was not predetermined.



Both sides were planned to have multiple narrative and political paths.



Autobot Leadership



The Autobots would have access to several possible leaders.



Choosing a leader would not simply provide different bonuses. Each leader was intended to represent a different philosophy regarding how the Autobot Commonwealth should be governed and how the conflict against the Decepticons should be approached.



Each potential leader would therefore have:



\- Unique political advantages

\- Unique disadvantages

\- Different strategic priorities

\- Different narrative events

\- Different relationships with other Cybertronian factions

\- Different approaches to the eventual global war



The intention was to make leadership a meaningful strategic and narrative decision rather than simply a statistical upgrade.



\---



Decepticon Internal Conflict



The Decepticon side was planned to contain an even more unstable political situation.



Although Megatron would initially dominate the movement, his leadership was not intended to be permanently guaranteed.



One planned scenario involved a full Decepticon Civil War.



Depending on player decisions and narrative developments, other figures could challenge Megatron and potentially take control of the Decepticon movement.



This would allow entirely different versions of the Decepticon Empire to emerge before the final confrontation with the Autobots.



As a result, a campaign could theoretically produce situations such as:

Autobot Leader C

&#x20;       vs

The final war would remain the central event, but the political circumstances surrounding it could change substantially between campaigns.



\---



The Final War



Regardless of the political path followed by either side, the growing rivalry between the Autobots and Decepticons was ultimately intended to become unsustainable.



Proxy warfare would eventually give way to direct global conflict.



The final stage of the campaign would therefore be a planet-wide war between the two dominant factions.



There would be no permanent compromise between them.



One side would eventually defeat the other.



The victorious faction would then unify Cybertron under its rule, serving as the conclusion of the campaign.



The entire game was therefore designed around escalation:

Experimental Nature of the Project



This project was always intended to be experimental.



Rather than attempting to create another conventional Hearts of Iron IV total conversion, the goal was to experiment with combining two concepts that are rarely the primary focus of the game at the same time:



Competitive 1v1 grand strategy



and



Interactive narrative storytelling



The strategic world would exist largely to support the evolving relationship between two major players.



Meanwhile, political paths, leaders, proxy wars, internal conflicts, and scripted events would provide the narrative framework surrounding that competition.



The intention was to create a campaign where two players could begin on opposite sides of Cybertron, develop radically different political histories, influence conflicts throughout the world, and eventually discover exactly what kind of Autobots and Decepticons would face each other in the inevitable final war.

Final Note



This mod began as an experiment: an attempt to build a Hearts of Iron IV experience where two enormous rival factions, interactive storytelling, proxy warfare, internal politics, and an inevitable final confrontation formed the core of the entire campaign.



Although the complete vision was never realized, the project established the foundations of its own Cybertron, including a custom map, political factions, ideologies, visual assets, interface work, and an original Transformers continuity created specifically for the setting.



Development from my side has ended, but the concept and existing work do not need to end with it.



Anyone interested in carrying the project forward is welcome to do so.
